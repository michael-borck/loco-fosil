## What this is

LocoFósil is a research project about hardware the modern AI stack has left behind. Not because the cards are interesting on their own, but because the floor is where most people actually are. A K1200 costs less than a new keyboard. An Optiplex costs less than a phone. If that combination can serve a useful 2B model at a usable speed and a sane wattage, that changes who can run local AI.

The shelf covers Kepler, Maxwell, and Pascal, from a 2 GB K620 to a 12 GB K6000. The inventory is on the [hardware page](/hardware/).

The project has four tracks: a compatibility matrix, a benchmark suite, a low-power serving node, and a lightweight harness. The first two are measurement. The last two are proof that the measurements mean something.
